Privacy Policy

Screwsmith · Last updated: 22 September 2026

TürkçeEnglish

In short

You don't need an account, a name or an email address to play Screwsmith, and the game plays offline. When you are online, two services run: ads (Google AdMob) and purchases (Apple, with RevenueCat for validation). Below is what each one processes and why.

What stays on your device

Your level, coins, tools and settings are stored on your phone and are not sent to us. To help balance the game, it writes events such as "level started / won / lost" to a log file on your device; that file never leaves it either. Deleting the game deletes all of it.

Ads — Google AdMob

Ads in the game are served by Google AdMob. Rewarded ads are always your choice; ads between levels only appear after level 30, rarely and in limited numbers. To show ads, Google may process: device identifiers (the advertising identifier only if you allow it), your IP address and the approximate location derived from it, ad interactions (impressions, taps) and diagnostic data used to measure ad performance.

  • Tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency): after the introduction levels your iPhone asks you once. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads are not personalised; nothing in the game changes. You can change your choice any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland: the game shows Google's consent form on first launch. You can change your choice in the game under Settings > "Ad privacy choices".
  • How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

The "No Ads" purchase removes the ads between levels for good.

Purchases — Apple and RevenueCat

Apple takes the payment; we never see your card details. To confirm a purchase is valid, a randomly generated anonymous ID and your purchase records (which product, when, in which country) are sent to RevenueCat. This ID is not linked to your name, email or advertising identifier. RevenueCat's privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy

What we don't collect

  • Name, email, phone number, contacts, photos
  • Precise location (GPS)
  • Health, financial or other sensitive personal information

We do not sell your data.

Permissions

The game does not ask for camera, microphone, location, contacts or notification access. The only permission it asks for is the tracking permission described above.

Children

The game's content is suitable for all ages, but it is not directed specifically at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

Deleting your data

Data on your device goes when you delete the game. To have the anonymous records held by the ad and purchase services deleted, write to alimertgulec38@gmail.com; we reply within 30 days.
