Privacy Policy

Screwsmith · Last updated: 16 September 2026

TürkçeEnglish

In short

Screwsmith does not collect, store or share any personal data. You don't need an account, an email address or an internet connection to play. The game runs entirely offline.

What stays on your device

Your level, coins, tools and settings are stored in your phone's own storage. They are never sent to a server and never reach us. Deleting the game deletes them.

To help with balancing, the game writes events such as "level started / won / lost" to a log file inside your device. That file never leaves your device, is never transmitted to us, and is removed when you delete the game.

What we do not collect

  • Name, email, phone number, contacts, photos
  • Location
  • Device or advertising identifiers
  • Usage or behavioural data

Ads and purchases

This version (1.0) contains no advertising and no in-app purchases. The whole game is free to play. If either is added in a future version, this policy will be updated first and every provider used will be named here.

Permissions

The game does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts, notification or tracking (App Tracking Transparency) permissions.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, children included.

Deleting your data

Everything on your device is removed when you delete the game. There is nothing for us to delete, because nothing is ever sent to us.

Changes
